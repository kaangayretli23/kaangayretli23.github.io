Hayır. Hesap açmanız gerekmez; oyun bize ya da başka bir şirkete veri göndermez. Ayrıntılar gizlilik politikasında.

About the game

Inflate a long balloon, twist it in the right spots, lock it and watch it turn into a dog. Squeaky latex, a lock that clicks every quarter turn, and a big POP if you overdo the air.

FAQ

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Not in this version. The game is free. If that changes, we will say so in the update notes and on this page.

Do you collect my data?

No. You don't need an account, and the game sends no data to us or any other company. See the privacy policy.

I lost my progress.

Progress (parties, coins, album) is stored only on your device. If you delete the app or choose "Reset progress" in Settings, it is gone; we have no servers, so we cannot restore it.

I can't turn the lock, the game feels too hard.

Turn on Settings → "Easy mode": the inflation band gets wider and a half turn is enough to lock. In Easy mode a party can earn at most 2 stars.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Email us with "Balloon Artist" in the subject line. Adding your phone model, iOS version and a screenshot helps us find the problem much faster.
