Balloon Artist Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

1. Who we are

Balloon Artist: Twist & Pop (Turkish name: Balon Ustası) is a mobile game made by İsmet Kaan Gayretli. Contact: tribunligi.app@gmail.com.

2. We collect no personal data

  • We do not ask you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, photos or contacts.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases.
  • The game sends no data to us or to any third party. It works without an internet connection.

3. What stays on your device

Your game progress (parties, coins, album, settings) is stored only on your device. Deleting the app, or "Reset progress" in the settings, removes this data.

4. What Apple collects

The App Store may collect data about downloads and app crashes under Apple's own privacy policy. Only if you turned on "Share with App Developers" on your device does Apple show us aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example, how many people return the next day). We cannot identify you from them. Details: Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Children

The game is made for all ages. Because we collect no personal data from anyone, we collect none from children either.

6. Changes

If ads or purchases are added in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand and the effective date will change.

7. Contact

Questions: tribunligi.app@gmail.com.
